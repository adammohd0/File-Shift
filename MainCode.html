What is FileShift?

FileShift is an advanced, 100% free, browser-native file conversion and online utility suite. Built with modern web standards and high-performance client-side JavaScript libraries, FileShift allows users to convert documents, spreadsheets, PDFs, and images instantly without relying on remote cloud servers.

Privacy Policy, Data Security & Compliance Terms

Effective Date: October 2026 • Last Updated for Cloudflare Deployment Standards.

1. Zero Server Uploads & Absolute Data Privacy

At FileShift, your privacy is mathematically guaranteed by our architecture. Because our conversion engine runs 100% client-side using browser-based JavaScript libraries (such as jsPDF, pdf-lib, Mammoth, and SheetJS), no files, text inputs, or passwords are ever uploaded, transmitted, logged, or stored on our hosting servers (Cloudflare Pages). Once you close or reload your browser tab, all transient file buffers are completely erased from your device's memory.
